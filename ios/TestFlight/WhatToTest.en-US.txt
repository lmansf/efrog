First TestFlight build of eFrog — frog call identification that runs entirely on your iPhone.

What to try:
• Analyze tab — tap Record and capture 5 seconds of a frog call (or play one from another device). The species and confidence appear when analysis finishes.
• Import an audio file (M4A, WAV, MP3) instead of recording, and analyze that.
• Tell us if the ID was right: Agree, Dispute (pick the correct species), or Not now.
• Collection tab — every analysis is saved here, newest first.
• Leaderboard tab — gem scores, ranked. Requires signing in.
• About tab — sign in with Auth0 so observations count toward the leaderboard across devices.

Known in this build:
• The species list covers 19 North American frogs and toads; anything else will be misidentified as the closest match.
• Audio is analyzed on-device and never uploaded — only the result is saved.

Please report: wrong identifications (with the species you expected), crashes, microphone permission trouble, and anything confusing in the flow.
